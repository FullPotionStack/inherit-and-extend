# Study and learning

Load for course selection, learning paths, or practice design.

## Sources and constraints

Start with the learner's previous work, syllabus, notes, available materials, and any real diagnostic results. If there are no results, say so. Establish the skill to learn, prerequisite knowledge, time budget, language/access needs, assessment goal, and whether feedback is available.

Inspect institution syllabi and publisher course/book pages for prerequisites, sequence, and exercises. Use learner reports to discover workload or accessibility problems, not to manufacture a consensus. Read learning research with attention to the task, participants, comparison condition, and retention interval.

## Comparison

Compare prerequisite fit, desired outcome, practice and feedback, workload, cost/access, and ability to change the plan. A complete advanced course can be a worse fit than selected introductory chapters; an easy resource may fail to cover the goal. Check sequence dependencies before moving chapters. Distinguish reading completion from recall, transfer, and independent problem solving.

The primary [Roediger and Karpicke experiment abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/) reports different outcomes for immediate versus delayed prose recall. The [Dunlosky et al. review abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26173288/) compares learning techniques and their generalizability. Neither validates a specific plan produced by this suite.

## Decision and review

Adopt fitting material, extend it with feedback, compose selected resources/practice, or build a narrow custom plan when no inspected path fits. State the limited learning target and a review point. Recommend a diagnostic or delayed task without claiming it has been administered.

Learning from a published sequence is distinct from copying its exercises, figures, answer keys, or teaching text. Link to the source; verify reuse terms before redistributing content.

Load the [illustrative statistics-path comparison](https://github.com/FullPotionStack/look-before-build/blob/main/docs/examples/study-path.md) only when a worked example is needed. It is repository documentation, not required for this reference to work in a standalone skill copy.
